# Trademarks and brand assets

The code in this repository is licensed under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). **The brand is not.**

Apache-2.0 grants copyright and patent rights. It explicitly does not grant trademark rights
(section 6), and nothing here should be read as granting them.

## Not covered by the code licence

- The name **Rot Royale**, and confusingly similar names.
- The logo and app icon, including every generated platform variant:
  - `frontend/assets/icon.png` — the source icon
  - `frontend/android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*/` — Android launcher icons
  - `frontend/ios/App/App/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/` — iOS app icon
  - `frontend/ios/App/App/Assets.xcassets/Splash.imageset/` — iOS splash
  - `frontend/public/icons/` — PWA and web icons
- The visual identity: the Starter theme's palette and typography as a combined get-up, the
  Rot Champion styling, and the score-ladder wording.

These files are in the repository because the native builds cannot produce a launchable app without
them. Their presence is a build requirement, not a licence.

## What you may do

- Use, modify and redistribute the **code**, under Apache-2.0.
- Say your work is *based on* or *derived from* Rot Royale, accurately and without implying
  endorsement or affiliation.
- Keep the icons in a private fork you build for yourself.

## What you may not do

- Publish an app called Rot Royale, or a name likely to be confused with it.
- Ship the logo, icon or splash in anything you distribute.
- Present a derivative as the official Rot Royale, or imply it is endorsed.

If you are distributing a fork, replace the icon assets listed above with your own. They are the
only files you need to change for the app to build under a different identity.

## Artwork that is not here

Most of the game's artwork is not in this repository at all — it is not published, and it is not
licensed for reuse. The placeholder images shipped here in its place are trivial generated panels
and carry no rights worth claiming.
